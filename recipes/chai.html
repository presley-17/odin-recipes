Chai Recipie

Description

Chai—specifically Masala Chai—is a deeply comforting, aromatic Indian tea beverage made by boiling black tea leaves in a blend of water, whole milk, and warm spices. Unlike Western tea which is gently steeped, chai is simmered vigorously on a stovetop to create a rich, creamy, and deeply flavorful brew.

Ingredients

  • Black tea
  • Milk
  • Sugar
  • Water

Steps

  1. Boil the spices
  2. Add tea and sugar
  3. Add milk and simmer

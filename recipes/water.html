Description

Water is a transparent, odorless, and tasteless chemical substance composed of two hydrogen atoms covalently bonded to one oxygen atom (\(H_{2}O\)). Covering roughly 71% of the Earth's surface, it is the foundational fluid for all known living organisms and ecosystems.

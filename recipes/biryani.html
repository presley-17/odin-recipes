Biryani Recipe lol

Description

Biryani is a celebrated South Asian mixed rice dish made with long-grain Basmati or indigenous short-grain rice, aromatic spices, and layered with spiced meat, fish, or vegetables. Slow-cooked in a sealed pot using the dum technique, it offers a fragrant, deeply flavorful experience deeply rooted in royal history.

Ingredients

  • Rice
  • Meat (Chicken or mutton) or Vegetables
  • Spices
  • Yogurt

Steps

  1. Marinate the protien
  2. Parboil the rice
  3. Cook the Base
  4. Layer the Pot
  5. Dum (Steam Cook)
